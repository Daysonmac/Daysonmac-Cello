About me

Hi, I'm an avid cellist with over 10 years of experience playing the cello now. I've participated in symphonies and ensembles nationally, and was an All-State cellist in high school. I'm familiar and taught in the Suzuki method, but love adding to my repertoire. Now, I'm looking to further my experience as a teacher by helping all aspiring cellists further their own talents at an affordable price, and grow together!!

What I teach

  • Foundations: posture, bow hold, shifting
  • Beginner repertoire & note-reading
  • Developing tone, rhythm, and basic music theory
  • Audition & recital preparation for early-level pieces
